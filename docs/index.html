Getting started

Enter one fixed-rate mortgage in USD using your lender statement. Enter the current principal, principal-and-interest payment, rate and payment dates. Taxes and insurance are shown separately.

What does the purchase unlock?

A one-time non-consumable lifetime purchase unlocks payment tracking, scenarios and progress. There is no subscription and there are no ads. Loan setup, the initial interest reveal and settings are accessible before purchase. Apple displays the price in your storefront currency.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account, then open Settings → One-time purchase → Restore purchases. Apple handles purchases and refund requests. A refund or revoked purchase removes access to paid tools without deleting your records.

Why is my balance different from my statement?

DayZero estimates fixed-rate monthly amortization. It excludes lender-specific daily interest, fees, variable rates and payment processing. Regular payments are not deducted automatically. Use Update balance from statement to reconcile principal and the next unpaid payment date. Dates on extra-payment records are for recordkeeping; historical transactions are not replayed as lender accounting. Displayed rounded rows may sum differently from unrounded estimates by a cent. This is a tracking and planning tool, not financial advice or a lender statement.

Back up and move your records

Settings → Back up my data exports a JSON file. Restore from a backup replaces current records after confirmation. Backups are unencrypted; store them securely. Deleting the app can erase local records. Optional private iCloud backup uses your Apple account and may take time to sync; keep file backups of important data, especially large histories.

Delete data

Settings → Delete loan and all records removes the local loan and payment history. Delete exported backups from their storage locations separately. Deleting local records does not erase an existing iCloud copy; manage iCloud data using your Apple account's iCloud storage controls. Disable the optional iCloud backup if you do not want future copies.

Widgets, reminders and languages

Widgets read the app's latest locally shared snapshot. Optional reminders require notification permission when enabled. Siri and Shortcuts provide a payoff forecast and open payment logging. English, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Vietnamese are supported; all mortgage amounts remain USD.

Reviews and updates

DayZero may request an App Store review after sufficient use and successful saved actions. Apple decides whether the review dialog appears; TestFlight does not display it. Settings offers a manual review link after the public listing is available. DayZero checks for public store updates and provides an official store link. iOS/App Store controls automatic installation. Beta updates are managed in TestFlight.
