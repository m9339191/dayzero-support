Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to the DayZero iOS app (com.deokjoomoon.dayzero), its widgets and shortcuts. Developer: Deokjoo Moon. Contact: it@bebriz.com.

Your mortgage records

Loan amounts, rates, dates, extra-payment history, notes and app preferences are stored on your device. DayZero does not require an app account or bank connection and does not send mortgage records to a developer-operated server. Widgets and shortcuts access a local App Group snapshot shared with DayZero.

Optional private iCloud backup

If you enable private iCloud backup, DayZero copies a backup of your records to Apple's iCloud key-value storage associated with your Apple account. Apple provides and processes this storage under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not operate the iCloud service or receive your backup. Synchronization is asynchronous and size-limited. Disabling backup stops future copies; deleting local records does not remove an existing cloud copy.

File backups and sharing

Backups, CSV/PDF exports and shared images are created when you request them. Backup files are not encrypted by DayZero. You choose their destination or recipient using system interfaces; the selected storage or sharing provider may process them under its own policy. Keep copies secure and delete exported files separately when no longer needed.

Purchases and App Store connections

Apple processes the optional one-time lifetime purchase and purchase restoration. DayZero uses StoreKit to verify ownership on the device; payment card details and Apple Account passwords are not provided to the developer. The app contacts Apple to load product prices and check the public app catalog for updates using the app ID and App Store region. These network requests expose normal connection information, such as IP address, to Apple. No loan values or payment notes are sent with these requests.

Reviews, reminders and local usage counters

Local counters store first use, session and successful-action counts, app version and the date of a review request attempt to avoid excessive prompts. DayZero does not collect review text or infer that a review was completed. Optional notification reminders are scheduled on your device after you grant permission.

No advertising or analytics

DayZero contains no advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics. The developer does not sell your records or collect their contents. Apple may provide diagnostics and store information under your Apple privacy settings.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives the address and information you choose to send, solely to respond to your request and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, lender statements or backup files. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address above, subject to necessary legal record retention.

Control and deletion

Manage or delete individual records in the app; Settings → Delete loan and all records clears the local mortgage history. You can disable reminders and optional iCloud backup. Remove exported files from their destinations and manage existing iCloud data separately in your Apple account. Uninstalling can erase local app data. Some local preference counters remain until app removal. System backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Children and changes

DayZero is a general-purpose mortgage tool and does not target children or knowingly collect children's personal information. We may update this policy when app behavior changes. The current effective date is shown above.
